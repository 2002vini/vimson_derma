Our Private Label Process

Quick, Clean, and Brand-Ready

At Vimson Derma, we don’t just manufacture, we bottle your brand’s brilliance. Our private label skincare and personal care manufacturing is built for speed, compliance, and confidence. No R&D wait times, no formulation hiccups - just market-ready products with your brand on it.

1

Product Pick & Play

Dive into our extensive catalog of ready-to-brand formulations from anti-acne serums and hydrating face creams to clarifying shampoos and soothing baby lotions. All dermatologist-tested, stability-approved, and made for rebranding.

2

Make It Yours: Branding & Packaging

Our design team helps you craft packaging that pops and choose from standard or luxe, minimalist or bold, all while staying compliant. Your brand identity takes center stage; we just dress it up to impress.

3

Labeling, Compliance & Paperwork

We handle the nitty-gritty like regulatory compliance, and labeling guidelines, so your products are shelf-ready and rule-proof. All necessary documentation is prepped and aligned for smooth sales.

4

PO to Production to Perfection

Once your Purchase Order (PO) is confirmed, we move to full-scale production at our certified facility in Gujarat. Each batch goes through strict quality checks to ensure your customers get consistent, safe, and effective products. No detours, no delays.

5

SO Long, Delays – It's Delivery Time

With your Sales Order (SO) in place, your branded products are packed, quality-stamped, and shipped. Whether that’s to your warehouse, retail outlet, or straight to your D2C fulfillment center. All that’s left? Watching your brand glow up.
